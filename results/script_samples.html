Farmer script samples

Every line below is what a farmer hears on the phone. Nothing is read; there are no digits anywhere, because a non-reader cannot tell whether “6:00” means morning or evening.

Ye scripts abhi verify nahi hue hain. Padhne wale se poochhna hai:

  1. Kya ye natural lagta hai?
  2. Kya koi shabd galat hai?
  3. Kya ek kisan bina padhe, sirf sunkar samajh lega?

1Night feeder, clock time given, field is dry

Power tonight 22:00 to 06:00. Run the pump from 22:00 for about 6h 49m.

EnglishReference. What the line is supposed to mean.

Ram, hello. Power is from ten o'clock at night to six o'clock in the morning. The wheat needs water. Start the pump at ten o'clock at night, and stop it at quarter to five in the morning. The field is dry. Press one to hear this again.
